WHO'S NADIEM?

An entrepreneur who believes technology can create better access to opportunities and transform the way people live and work.

Nadiem Makarim is an Indonesian entrepreneur and the co-founder of Gojek, one of Southeast Asia's first decacorn startups. He later served as Minister of Education, Culture, Research, and Technology, leading the "Merdeka Belajar" reform. His journey combines technology, entrepreneurship, and social impact.

Nadiem, 2022

Mendirikan Gojek pada tahun 2010 dan turut membesarkannya menjadi salah satu perusahaan teknologi terbesar di Asia Tenggara.
